## What the Report Analyzes

- Revenue by product category and city
- Revenue across 2021, 2022, and 2023
- Monthly revenue and quantity sold
- Revenue by day of the week
- Revenue versus profit by category, month, and city

The dashboards include filters for year, city, and month.

## Workbook Structure

| Sheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `KPI Actual` | KPI calculations |
| `Coffee Shop Data` | Source data |
| `Analysis ` | Supporting analysis |
| `DASHBOARD` | Sales and revenue overview |
| `DASHBOARD (2)` | Revenue versus profit comparisons |
| `INSIGHTS` | Written findings |

## Selected Findings

- **Desserts** generated the highest category revenue ($7,204) and profit ($2,522) in the displayed view.
- **Chicago** had the highest city revenue ($11,206) and profit ($3,922).
- **January** had the highest monthly revenue ($4,544).
- In the weekly chart, **Saturday** had the highest displayed revenue (7,039), followed by **Friday** (7,011). **Wednesday** had the lowest (6,208).

These figures describe the displayed dashboard view. Check the selected filters before using them in another report.

## How to Use

1. [Download the Excel workbook](coffe%20and%20food%20business%20report.xlsx).
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3. Use `DASHBOARD` to explore revenue and sales patterns.
4. Open `DASHBOARD (2)` to compare revenue with profit.
5. Change the year, city, or month filters to inspect specific segments.
6. Read `INSIGHTS` alongside the dashboards and verify the active filters before citing a figure.
