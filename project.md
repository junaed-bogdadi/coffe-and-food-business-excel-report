# Coffee and Food Business Report Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Coffee and Food Business Report Dashboard** created using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard analyzes business performance based on revenue, profit, quantity sold, transactions, product categories, city-wise sales, yearly trends, monthly trends, and weekly sales performance.

The main objective of this project is to convert raw coffee and food sales data into meaningful business insights. This dashboard helps users understand which products, cities, months, and sales periods contribute most to overall business performance.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights the following major KPIs:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | $46,374 |
| Total Profit | $16,231 |
| Total Transactions | 9,971 |
| Total Quantity Sold | 14,117 |

These KPIs provide a quick summary of the overall business performance.

---

## Dashboard 1: Revenue and Sales Overview

The first dashboard provides an overview of revenue performance across product categories, cities, years, months, and weekly trends.

### Revenue by Product Categories

The dashboard analyzes revenue across different product categories:

- Desserts
- Sandwiches
- Tea Beverages
- Coffee Beverages
- Specialty Drinks
- Snacks
- Cold Drinks

Desserts generated the highest revenue, followed by Sandwiches and Tea Beverages. This indicates that dessert items are the strongest revenue driver for the business.

---

### Revenue by City

City-wise revenue analysis shows performance across five cities:

| City | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Chicago | 11,206 |
| Los Angeles | 10,787 |
| New York | 10,425 |
| Austin | 8,368 |
| Seattle | 5,587 |

Chicago is the top-performing city in terms of revenue, while Seattle generated the lowest revenue among the listed cities.

---

### Revenue by Year-over-Year

The dashboard compares yearly revenue for 2021, 2022, and 2023. The values are close to each other, which indicates relatively stable year-wise revenue performance.

---

### Revenue by Monthly Sales Trend

The monthly trend shows revenue and quantity sold from January to December. January generated the highest revenue, while February and September showed comparatively lower performance.

This monthly analysis helps identify seasonal sales patterns and supports better sales planning.

---

### Revenue by Weekly Trend Analysis

The weekly trend analysis shows that sales increased toward the end of the week.

Key weekly insight:

- Highest sales day: Friday
- Second-highest sales day: Saturday
- Lowest sales day: Sunday

This indicates stronger customer demand on Fridays and Saturdays.

The second dashboard focuses on comparing revenue and profit across categories, months, and cities.

### Month-wise Revenue vs Profit

The monthly revenue vs profit chart helps compare business performance throughout the year.

January generated the highest revenue and profit, while February and September were among the lower-performing months.

This section helps management understand which months need stronger promotional activities.

---

### City-wise Revenue vs Profit

The city-wise revenue vs profit chart compares performance across different cities.

| City | Revenue | Profit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Chicago | 11,206 | 3,922 |

## Dashboard 3: Insights Report

The third dashboard page presents a written summary of the key findings from the analysis.

### Main Insights

1. **The business shows strong overall performance.**  
   Total revenue is $46,374, total profit is $16,231, total transactions are 9,971, and total quantity sold is 14,117.

2. **Desserts are the top revenue-generating product category.**  
   Desserts generated the highest revenue and also produced the highest profit.

3. **Chicago is the strongest city.**  
   Chicago generated the highest revenue and profit among all cities.

4. **Seattle is the lowest-performing city.**  
   Seattle generated the lowest revenue and profit, which creates an opportunity for improvement.

5. **Sales are stronger on Fridays and Saturdays.**  
   Weekly trend analysis shows that sales increase toward the end of the week.

6. **January is the best-performing month.**  
   January recorded the highest monthly revenue.

7. **Profit distribution follows revenue patterns.**  
   Cities and categories with higher revenue also generated higher profit.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard insights, the following recommendations can be made:

1. Focus more on **Desserts**, as this category generates the highest revenue and profit.
2. Increase marketing and promotional activities in **Chicago**, **Los Angeles**, and **New York**.
3. Improve sales strategies for **Seattle**, as it is the lowest-performing city.
4. Run special offers during lower-performing months such as February and September.
5. Use weekend-focused campaigns because sales are stronger on Fridays and Saturdays.
6. Maintain product variety because revenue is distributed across multiple product categories.
7. Track revenue and profit together to ensure that sales growth also improves profitability.

## Conclusion

The Coffee and Food Business Report Dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of business performance. It helps users analyze revenue, profit, transactions, quantity sold, product categories, cities, months, years, and weekly sales trends.

The dashboard shows that Desserts are the strongest product category, Chicago is the top-performing city, and sales perform better toward the end of the week. This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used as a practical business intelligence tool for analyzing sales data and generating actionable insights.
